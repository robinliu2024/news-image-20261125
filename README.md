Sum up 25 November's world news in one image: a newspaper front page of Wednesday 25 November 2026 — Huawei found guilty on 10 of 12 counts by a Brooklyn jury, Shanghai party chief Chen Jining under investigation, gunmen holding 25 hostage in a Cartagena supermarket, a Hanoi hotpot blast killing 6, Russian jets entering South Korea's air defence zone, Senegal's Africa Cup title restored by CAS, and a Russian strike killing 2 in Odesa. An AI judge compares the images two at a time and ranks them by how well they sum up the day's news.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

This copies `entry.png` (1024×683, under 1 MiB) to `/output/entry.png`, byte for byte, so every run writes identical output. No network access.
